Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Disaster Ready handles information when you use the app. Disaster Ready is designed to keep your personal preparedness information on your device and to minimize the information shared with external services.

Information stored on your device

Disaster Ready may store information that you enter or configure, including your household profile, emergency plans, personal meeting or alternative-location notes, contacts, important phone numbers, preparedness supplies, household roles, preferences, and Payment Preparedness checklist completion.

This information is stored locally on your device using Apple's local storage technologies. Disaster Ready does not provide a user account and does not upload this preparedness information to the developer.

Location

Location access is optional. Disaster Ready requests a single location reading only when you choose Use My Location. It does not continuously track your location and does not use background location tracking.

Your location may be used to calculate approximate distances to public civil-defence shelters or to determine whether an official MET Norway weather warning covers your selected location. These calculations are performed on your device.

Your latitude and longitude are not stored in Disaster Ready's database, caches, backups, or location history, and Disaster Ready does not add your coordinates to requests sent to MET Norway or Geonorge.

Official weather warnings

Disaster Ready uses MET Norway's MetAlerts service to retrieve official weather-warning information. The app downloads national warning information and geographic warning areas and performs location matching on your device.

Requests to MET Norway are made directly from your device. Like ordinary internet requests, MET Norway may receive network information such as your public IP address, request time, and technical HTTP information. Disaster Ready does not include your device location, household information, contacts, plans, or payment checklist information in these requests.

Public civil-defence shelters

Public civil-defence-shelter information is obtained from official DSB data distributed through Geonorge.

Disaster Ready downloads official shelter reference information and calculates approximate distance on your device. Your location is not included in the request to Geonorge.

Shelter information is reference information only. A nearby shelter is not automatically the correct shelter to use. Always follow current instructions from Norwegian public authorities.

Apple MapKit and Apple Maps

If you manually search for an area for weather warnings, the search text may be sent to Apple's MapKit service so Apple can resolve the place to a geographic location. Disaster Ready does not retain a history of these searches.

If you choose View on Map for a public shelter, Disaster Ready opens Apple Maps with the selected shelter's official coordinates and available name or address. Disaster Ready does not add your current location or automatically request navigation. Apple's services are governed by Apple's own privacy practices.

Payment Preparedness

Payment Preparedness stores only the country setting and which preparedness checklist items you have marked complete.

Disaster Ready does not request or store bank account numbers, card numbers, PIN codes, BankID credentials, passwords, account balances, or cash amounts.

Contacts

Family contacts and important numbers are entered manually inside Disaster Ready and stored locally. Disaster Ready does not access or import your iPhone address book and does not upload these contacts.

When you choose to call or message a number, the selected number is handed to iOS using the normal Phone or Messages functionality. Disaster Ready does not store resulting call or message history.

Backups

Backup export and import are initiated by you. A Disaster Ready backup may contain household information, contacts, important numbers, emergency plans and personal planning notes, household roles, and supply information.

Backups do not contain your current or historical device location, MET or shelter caches, or banking credentials.

You choose where an exported backup is stored using Apple's Files interface. The destination may be local storage, iCloud Drive, or another file provider you have configured. Once exported, that file is outside Disaster Ready's app container and is subject to the privacy and retention practices of the storage provider you choose.

Notifications

Disaster Ready may use local notifications for preparedness reminders when you enable them. These reminders are scheduled on your device.

Disaster Ready does not use a remote push-notification service and does not obtain or store a push-notification device token.

External websites and services

Disaster Ready provides links to official information from public authorities such as DSB, MET Norway, NVE, Helsenorge, Politiet, and Geonorge.

When you choose to open an external website, your browser connects directly to that service. The external service may receive ordinary network information such as your public IP address and technical request information. Disaster Ready does not control the privacy, security, or retention practices of external services.

Analytics, advertising and tracking

Disaster Ready does not include advertising SDKs, analytics SDKs, tracking SDKs, cross-app tracking, App Tracking Transparency requests, or advertising-identifier use.

Data retention and deletion

Information stored inside Disaster Ready remains on your device until you edit or remove it, use an applicable reset function, or delete the app. Some preferences may remain until the app itself is deleted.

Temporary reference caches may be removed automatically by iOS. Exported backup files are not deleted when you remove data from Disaster Ready; you must separately delete exported files from the location where you saved them.

Children's privacy

Disaster Ready does not provide child accounts or a child-directed data collection service. A household profile can record whether children are part of the household, and plans or contacts may contain information entered by the user about household members. This information remains locally stored unless the user explicitly exports a backup.

Safety and official information

Disaster Ready is a preparedness planning tool. It does not replace current instructions from emergency services or public authorities. Official instructions should always take priority during an emergency.

Changes to this Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when Disaster Ready changes or when privacy practices or legal requirements change. The latest version will be published on this page with an updated effective date.
