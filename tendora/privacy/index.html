Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Tendora handles information when you use the app.

Information we collect

Tendora is designed to collect only information needed to provide its features. The exact data used depends on the features you choose.

Data you enter

Tendora may allow you to store information about your home, assets, maintenance tasks, notes, dates, photos, or similar content. Unless the app clearly tells you otherwise, this information is intended to remain under your control and is not sold to third parties.

Photos and device access

If you choose to add photos or files, Tendora may request access to your photo library, camera, or files. Access is used only to provide the feature you choose to use.

Analytics and diagnostics

The app may receive limited diagnostic or crash information through Apple or other service providers if such features are enabled. This information is used to improve reliability and performance.

Third-party services

Some features may rely on third-party services. When a third-party service is used, information required to perform that feature may be processed according to that provider's own privacy policy.

Data sharing

We do not sell your personal information. Information may be shared only when necessary to provide a feature you request, comply with law, protect rights or safety, or with service providers acting on our behalf.

Data retention

Information stored locally remains on your device until you delete it, remove the app, or otherwise clear the data. Information processed by third-party services may be retained according to their applicable policies.

Children

Tendora is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law.

Your choices

You can control app permissions through iOS Settings. You may also delete locally stored app data by using in-app controls where available or by deleting the app.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The latest version will be published on this page with an updated date.
